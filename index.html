About

My research asks how generative models can represent meaningful clinical change, rather than simply edit the appearance of a medical image.

I am an M.S. student in Computer Science and Engineering at POSTECH. I received my B.S. in Computer Engineering from Chungbuk National University and studied as an exchange student at the University of Wolverhampton.

My long-term goal is to develop AI systems that help clinicians reason about disease progression, intervention, and patient-specific futures with clearer evidence and stronger structural consistency.
