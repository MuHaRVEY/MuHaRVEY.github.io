My story

My interest in AI became a serious path in 2024, when I began turning ideas into systems built around real human needs.

Through ICCAS & EKC 2024 , I worked with my team to develop an Alzheimer’s-prevention mobile game and presented it at the University of Warwick. At CEDC 2024 , I proposed a stroke-prevention and rehabilitation game using speech recognition and an EMG sensor. These projects showed me that technical work becomes meaningful when it starts from the people it is intended to support.

Those experiences led me to pursue graduate study. I am now an M.S. student at the Medical AI Lab at POSTECH, advised by Prof. Sang Hyun Park. I am still exploring my research direction, with particular interest in generative models and explainable, clinically grounded Medical AI.
