About Research CV

Research

Generative models that encode clinically meaningful change.

My work focuses on diffusion and flow-matching models for medical imaging, with particular interest in explicit clinical conditioning, interpretability, and subject-specific consistency over time.

Research question

How can a generative model simulate a plausible patient-specific future while making the clinical assumptions behind that future explicit and testable?

01

Current direction

2026 — Present

Expert-informed counterfactual follow-up MRI generation

Ongoing

A framework for generating longitudinal brain MRI under structured expert priors, designed to preserve patient identity and anatomical continuity while representing clinically specified disease progression.

Problem

Text-only editing is too ambiguous for region-specific clinical hypotheses and future-state simulation.

Approach

Translate expert observations into structured clinical variables and regional volumetric priors.

Modeling

Study diffusion and flow-matching backbones with explicit conditioning and structural controls.

Evaluation

Measure anatomy, subject preservation, temporal consistency, and sensitivity to clinical conditions.

BrLP reproduction & conditioning study

Reproduced diagnosis- and age-conditioned brain MRI generation; evaluated regional volume trajectories and compared full ControlNet against an ablated setting. This work established the experimental basis for introducing structured clinician priors.

Decision modeling for sleep prediction

As a research intern at KAIST, studied an existing Drift Diffusion Model pipeline, transformed rodent and wearable sleep data for model input, and visualized model outputs.
